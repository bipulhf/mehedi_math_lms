# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The mobile app is for Bangla-first school and college students studying maths on their phones. Teachers are a secondary audience who need to review course material, messages, exams, and marking work away from a computer.

## Product Purpose

Mehedi's Math Academy gives students one place to discover courses, study lessons, sit exams, track progress, pay for enrolments, and contact teachers. The mobile experience must make the next useful learning action obvious without hiding the course detail a student needs to trust it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, and encouraging. The interface should feel like a quiet classroom that supports long study sessions, not a loud social feed or a generic dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified noise, crowded card grids, decorative gradients, heavy shadows, rainbow status colours, and motion that delays a task. Do not make the app resemble a finance dashboard, a social network, or a marketing landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next learning action in clear reach.
2. Preserve attention with quiet surfaces and restrained colour.
3. Keep Bangla text comfortable to read and never force it into narrow fixed-width blocks.
4. Let course progress, payments, and exam state explain themselves in words as well as colour.
5. Keep the mobile flow familiar, direct, and useful on a small screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast requirements. Give every interactive control an accessible label and role where React Native does not provide one. Keep touch targets at least 44 by 44 points, support TalkBack and VoiceOver, and avoid nonessential motion so the app remains comfortable for students with reduced-motion needs.
